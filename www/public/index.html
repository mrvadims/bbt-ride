How it works

  1. 1

    See what's planned

    One list of everything coming up, with the meeting place, the route and a weather check for the start time.

  2. 2

    Say you're in

    Tap In or Out so everyone knows who's coming, and talk it over in the activity's thread.

  3. 3

    Never miss a change

    Notifications for new activities, changes and cancellations, and a calendar link that keeps your calendar up to date.
