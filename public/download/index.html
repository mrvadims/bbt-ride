BBT for Android

The team app, with notifications on your phone.

Download BBT for Android

The Android app isn't available yet. Use BBT in your browser at app.bbt.team for now.

Install

  1. On your Android phone, tap Download BBT for Android.
  2. Open the downloaded file. The first time, Android asks to allow your browser to install apps: allow it, then go back and tap Install.
  3. Open BBT and sign in the way you always do (Google or password).
  4. Turn on notifications when the app asks. Choose which sports you hear about in the account menu.

When there's a new version, the app shows Update available; it brings you back here.

About the app

It's the same BBT as in your browser, with the phone's own notifications (including Still in / I'm out buttons), Google sign-in and share sheet. The app contains no team data and sign-in is still by invitation. It isn't in the Google Play Store, which is why Android asks before installing it. See Privacy.

iPhone

Open app.bbt.team in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Open BBT from the Home Screen and turn on notifications in the account menu.
